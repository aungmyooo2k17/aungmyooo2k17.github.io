**Information Collection and Use**

Asset Ledger is a personal, offline record-keeping app for tracking assets such as cars and properties, their costs, income, photos, and profit. All information you enter into the app — including ledger entries, amounts, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your own device. I do not collect, transmit, sell, or have access to this information in any way. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no server that receives your data.

The app does not contain advertising, and it does not include analytics or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

**Photos and Camera**

The app lets you attach photos (for example, proof of costs or pictures of an asset) to your records. To do this, the app may ask permission to use your camera or to select images from your device. These photos are stored only on your device together with your other app data. They are not uploaded to me or to any third party.

**Backups**

The app provides an optional, manually triggered backup feature that bundles your database and photos into a single file. When you use a destination such as Google Drive, that file is stored in your own account and is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy — not by me. I never receive a copy of your backups.
